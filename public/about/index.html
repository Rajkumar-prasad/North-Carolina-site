How it works

A free service that connects homeowners with independent local garage door providers.

GarageDoorRepairServices.online is a referral service. We do not repair or install garage doors ourselves. When you call the number on this site, your call is routed to an independent garage door provider that serves your area.

What happens when you call

  1. You call and describe your garage door problem and your location.
  2. You are connected with an independent provider who serves your ZIP code.
  3. You arrange a visit and agree any price directly with that provider.

What we do and don't do

  • Using this service is free for homeowners.
  • Providers are independent businesses. We do not employ them and do not warrant or guarantee their work.
  • Availability depends on provider participation and location.
  • Before work begins, ask the provider for proof of insurance, any license required for the job, and a written price.

Our guides

We publish plain-English garage door guides so you can understand the problem before you call - including which jobs are safe to check yourself and which should always be left to a technician.
