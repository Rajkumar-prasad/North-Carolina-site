Garage Door Off Track: Causes & Safe Next Steps

What it means when a garage door comes off its track, what usually causes it, and why it needs a professional repair.

Call (555) 000-0000

A garage door rides up and down on rollers that sit inside two metal tracks. When one or more rollers pop out of the track, the door can hang crooked, jam partway, or tilt dangerously. An off-track door is unstable, so it needs attention quickly.

Signs the door is off track

  • The door hangs at an angle or one side is higher than the other.
  • Rollers are visibly outside the track or the track is bent.
  • The door jams, scrapes or makes grinding noises.
  • A gap appears between the door and the track on one side.
Safety first: Stop using the door and the opener. Unplug the opener so nobody presses the button. An off-track door can fall, and the springs and cables are still under tension.

Common causes

  • Bumping the door with a car - a common cause, especially when backing out before the door is fully open.
  • A broken lift cable or spring - when one side loses support, the door twists and the rollers jump out.
  • Worn or broken rollers - old plastic or steel rollers can crack and slip out of the track.
  • Loose or bent tracks - brackets loosen over time and tracks drift out of alignment.

What a technician will do

The technician will secure the door, release tension where needed, straighten or replace damaged track sections, reset the rollers, and replace any broken cables or rollers. They should finish by checking the door's balance and the opener's safety settings.

Can I push it back myself?

Small adjustments to a slightly loose track bracket can be done by a careful homeowner, but a door that is tilted, jammed or has slack cables should be left to a professional. Cables and springs work together, and forcing the door can make the damage worse.
