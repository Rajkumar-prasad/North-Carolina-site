Garage Door Opener Not Working: Troubleshooting Guide

Simple checks for a garage door opener that won't respond, hums or clicks, plus signs it is time for repair or a new opener.

Call (555) 000-0000

When the opener stops working, the cause is often simple - but sometimes the opener is worn out or the problem is actually the door itself. Here is how to narrow it down.

Step-by-step checks

  1. Power. Make sure the opener is plugged in and the outlet works. Check for a tripped breaker or GFCI outlet in the garage.
  2. Remote vs wall button. If the wall button works but the remote doesn't, replace the remote battery or re-pair the remote using the opener's learn button.
  3. Lock mode. Check whether the wall control is set to lock or vacation mode.
  4. Safety sensors. Misaligned or dirty sensors will stop the door from closing. Look for steady lights on both sensors.
  5. Disconnected trolley. If someone pulled the red emergency release cord, the motor will run but the door won't move until the trolley is reconnected.

What the sounds can tell you

  • Humming but the door doesn't move: could be a stripped gear, a disconnected trolley, or a door that is too heavy because of a broken spring.
  • Clicking only: often an electrical or logic board issue.
  • Grinding: worn gears or a chain or belt problem.
Check the springs first. If the door is very heavy or a spring looks broken, don't keep running the opener - it is not designed to lift the full weight of the door and you may burn out the motor.

Repair or replace the opener?

Older openers without modern safety sensors, units that need repeated repairs, or openers that are very loud are good candidates for replacement. Newer models can be quieter and may offer smartphone control and battery backup.
