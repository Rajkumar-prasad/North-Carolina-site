Privacy policy

How this website handles information about visitors and callers.

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Information we collect

This website does not require you to create an account or submit personal details. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.

Phone calls

When you call a number on this website, the call is handled by a call-tracking and routing partner, which connects you with an independent local service provider. Your phone number, call time, call duration and general location may be recorded, and calls may be recorded for quality and verification purposes. The provider you are connected with will have your phone number and any details you share on the call.

How information is used

  • To connect you with a local service provider.
  • To verify and measure calls.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

Cookies and analytics

If we add analytics or similar tools, they may use cookies to measure how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Third parties

Independent service providers have their own privacy practices, which are not covered by this policy.
