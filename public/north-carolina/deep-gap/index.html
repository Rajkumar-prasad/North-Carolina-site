What to ask before work begins

Every provider in our network is an independent business. These questions help you choose with confidence.

  • Proof of insuranceAsk for liability insurance before anyone works on springs or installs a door.
  • Any license required locallyCheck what license, if any, your town or county requires for the job.
  • A written, itemized priceGet the price for parts and labor in writing before work begins.
  • Warranty detailsAsk what is covered on parts and on labor, and for how long.
  • Clear explanationA good technician explains what failed and why, and shows you the worn part.

Never DIY a spring

Garage door springs hold enough tension to cause serious injury. If a spring is broken, keep the door closed, unplug the opener, and let a trained technician handle it.

The same goes for lift cables and the bottom brackets they attach to.
